# Official data-access requests

These are draft letters only. They were not sent. They are the non-code half of the official facility-registry phase: public directories can seed names and some addresses, but current doctor postings and bulk PM&DC matching still need written access.

Use the organisation’s own letterhead, add a contact person, and do not imply that Sehat Directory Pakistan is a government project.

---

## 1. Provincial Health Department / HRMIS

**To:** Secretary Health / Director General Health Services  
**Subject:** Request for licensed access to the current government health-facility list and current medical-officer posting data

We operate an independent, unofficial public directory that helps intending Hajj pilgrims find government medical facilities. The directory does not publish a doctor until current government employment, a trusted valid PM&DC match, a verified facility, and Hajj-attestation eligibility are all established.

Public seniority lists and transfer PDFs are not sufficient to prove a current posting. We therefore request, if your department can grant it:

1. The current official master list of government health facilities, including name, type, district, tehsil, address, phone, and operational status.
2. A licensed extract or read-only arrangement for current Medical Officer / specialist postings (name, father’s name, designation, current facility, district, and posting date).
3. Written terms of use, update frequency, and a named official contact.

We will not scrape login-protected HRMIS systems or bypass access controls. Data would be used only to help the public locate government facilities and, where fully verified, doctors who may attest Hajj medical forms under the Ministry of Religious Affairs rules then in force.

## 2. Pakistan Medical and Dental Council

**To:** Registrar / IT Section, Pakistan Medical and Dental Council  
**Email:** it.section@pmdc.pk  
**Subject:** Request for licensed soft-copy extract of the public practitioner register

The PM&DC public search at pmdc.pk is used for manual verification only. Name and father’s name do not reliably uniquely identify a practitioner, and there is no documented public API.

Registration Regulations 2023 state that data of registered practitioners may be provided on request in soft copy on payment. We request a licensed extract or official matching arrangement that includes:

- full name
- father’s name
- registration number
- registration status and validity
- registered qualifications

We will not guess registration numbers, scrape the public search, or bypass authentication. Trusted PMDC numbers would be stored only after an explicit verified match and would be shown publicly only under the directory’s existing publication rules.

## 3. Ministry of Religious Affairs and Interfaith Harmony

**To:** Ministry of Religious Affairs and Interfaith Harmony  
**Subject:** Request for the current official Hajj medical-certificate instructions

Please confirm when the official Hajj 2027 medical certificate and any accompanying guidelines will be published on mora.gov.pk, and whether the certifying practitioner must be:

- a registered medical practitioner, and/or
- a government doctor at a civil or Armed Forces medical facility.

Until that document is published, this directory will not label any doctor as Hajj-attestation verified.
